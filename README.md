A newspaper front page summing up 17 November 2026's world news in one 1024×1024 image: 41 stories, each verified against at least two sources as happening or first reported on Tuesday 17 November 2026, across six continents — with three illustrated lead stories, ten illustrated cards, a world map, and a day-in-figures strip. Contested claims are marked as claims. `render.py` documents how the image was drawn; the reviewed image ships as base64 text in `entry.png.b64`, and `main.py` decodes it to the output path.
